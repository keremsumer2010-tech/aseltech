# Konya Plain, Türkiye (`konya_plain`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (PC5 review; listed as `medium` in `atlas.json` pending off-basin rerun)

- **Why it is sinking:** Agricultural groundwater extraction from unconsolidated alluvial-lacustrine aquifers causes clay-interbed compaction and sinkhole collapse ([Üstün et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.5194/nhess-10-1151-2010); [Orhan et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/s21030774)).
- **Who is affected:** Irrigation districts, rural roads, canals, and farming towns across Konya Province.
- **Published rate:** −1.2 to −5.2 cm/yr regional GNSS; >−7.0 cm/yr in farming bowls ([Üstün et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.5194/nhess-10-1151-2010); [Orhan et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/s21030774)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary):** −8.9 cm/100d LOS (−9.6 cm LOS, −12.1 cm vertical est., 2026-06-26 to 2026-10-12, 8 pairs; 11.6% valid pixels, ref sensitivity [−14.0, −9.6] cm). Peak summer pumping, 1.5° × 0.7° bounding box, and low coherence make this preliminary.
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band coherence over dry agricultural soil captures drawdown gradients across the wider plain.
