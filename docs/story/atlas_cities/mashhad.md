# Mashhad, Iran (`mashhad`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Medium

- **Why it is sinking:** Heavy agricultural and urban groundwater extraction lowers hydraulic head by >60 m, driving irreversible compaction of compressible Quaternary clay-silt aquitards in an elongated fault-bounded valley ([Motagh et al., 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-246X.2006.03246.x)).
- **Who is affected:** 3+ million residents in Iran's second-largest city, international transit links, railways, and municipal water infrastructure threatened by ground fissures.
- **Published rate:** 20 to 30 cm/yr peak Envisat InSAR and leveling rates (up to 90 cm cumulative drop, 1995–2005; [Motagh et al., 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-246X.2006.03246.x)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary):** −7.17 cm/100d LOS (−8.6 cm LOS, −11.3 cm vertical est., 2026-06-19 to 2026-10-17, 120 days, 9 provisional pairs; 70.5% valid pixels, ref range [−8.6, −6.0] cm).
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band phase stability maintains 70.5% coherence across the semi-arid plain, mapping the full NW–SE subsidence trough in a single standardized pass.
