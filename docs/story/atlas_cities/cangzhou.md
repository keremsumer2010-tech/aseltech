# Cangzhou, China (`cangzhou`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Medium (`43.2%` valid)

- **Why it is sinking:** Long-term deep confined groundwater extraction across the North China Plain compacts thick Quaternary silty-clay aquitards beneath Cangzhou ([Zhang et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12665-013-2532-7)).
- **Who is affected:** Urban districts, Bohai Sea coastal flood defenses, irrigated farmland, and the Beijing–Shanghai High-Speed Railway corridor.
- **Published rate:** Historically −5 to −10 cm/yr (>2 m cumulative since 1970), moderating to ~−2 to −4 cm/yr after pumping restrictions and South-to-North Water Diversion ([Zhang et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12665-013-2532-7)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary):** −2.0 cm/100d LOS (−2.4 cm LOS, −3.2 cm vertical est., 2026-06-21 to 2026-10-19, 120 days, 8 pairs; 43.2% valid pixels; ref sensitivity [−2.4, −1.6] cm).
- **What NISAR adds:** 24 cm L-band radar tracks residual alluvial basin settlement across irrigated cropland and high-speed rail corridors every 12 days.
