# Hanoi, Vietnam (`hanoi`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Municipal and industrial pumping lowers piezometric heads in the deeper Pleistocene aquifer, compacting 50–90 m thick compressible Quaternary deltaic sediments (Dang et al., 2014).
- **Who is affected:** Southern and western Red River districts (Hoang Mai, Ha Dong, Thanh Xuan, Hoai Duc), urban housing, drainage canals, and flood-defense embankments.
- **Published rate:** Up to −6.8 cm/yr vertical (ALOS InSAR, 2007–2011), moderating or shifting outward to −0.5 to −0.8 cm/yr in suburban bowls (2016–2020; Dang et al., 2014).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary, indicative only):** −2.1 cm/100d LOS (−2.5 cm LOS, −3.6 cm vertical est., 2026-06-19 to 2026-10-17, 9 pairs; ref sensitivity [−6.7, −2.2] cm).
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band maintains better signal through subtropical vegetation and monsoon humidity than C-band, though reference sensitivity keeps this 120-day estimate indicative.
