# Tehran, Iran (`tehran`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Unregulated agricultural and industrial groundwater pumping dewaters quaternary alluvial gravels and compressible lacustrine clay-silt layers across the southwest Tehran and Shahriar plains ([Haghighi & Motagh, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2019.01.030); [Mahmoudpour et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enggeo.2016.03.003)).
- **Who is affected:** 9+ million metropolitan residents, international transit corridors, southern railway mainlines, and extensive irrigation infrastructure threatened by earth fissures.
- **Published rate:** −15 to >−20 cm/yr regional average; −25 to −36 cm/yr localized maxima in the Shahriar and Varamin agricultural funnels ([Haghighi & Motagh, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2019.01.030)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary):** −2.92 cm/100d LOS (−3.5 cm LOS, −4.7 cm vertical est., 2026-06-18 to 2026-10-16, 120 days; 36.4% valid pixels). An in-basin primary reference depressed the headline; referencing northern Alborz bedrock yields −7.3 cm LOS (−9.7 cm vertical est.).
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band phase stability tracks deformation continuity across the arid suburban fringe where ground cracks cut active farmland.
