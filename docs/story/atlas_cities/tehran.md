# Tehran, Iran (`tehran`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Unregulated groundwater pumping dewaters alluvial gravels and compressible lacustrine clay-silt layers across southwest Tehran and Shahriar plains ([Haghighi & Motagh, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2019.01.030); [Mahmoudpour et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enggeo.2016.03.003)).
- **Who is affected:** 9+ million residents, transit corridors, southern railways, and irrigation networks threatened by ground fissures.
- **Published rate:** −15 to >−20 cm/yr regional average; −25 to −36 cm/yr in Shahriar and Varamin agricultural funnels ([Haghighi & Motagh, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2019.01.030)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary):** −6.2 cm/100d LOS (−7.4 cm LOS, −9.8 cm vertical est., 2026-06-18 to 2026-10-16, 120 days; 38.1% valid pixels; ref sensitivity [−7.4, −2.2] cm). Recalculated with Alborz bedrock reference; previous in-basin reference gave −3.5 cm.
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band phase stability tracks deformation continuity across arid suburban margins where ground cracks cut active farmland.
