# Central Valley (Tulare Basin), USA (`central_valley`)

**Status:** Press-approved (PC5) · **Confidence:** Medium

- **Why it is sinking:** Intensive groundwater pumping for irrigated agriculture drains confined clay-rich aquitards in the Corcoran Clay, causing irreversible inelastic compaction ([Neely et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020WR028451); [Ojha et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1029/2018JB016083)).
- **Who is affected:** Major water-conveyance canals (Friant-Kern, California Aqueduct), bridges, railways, and farming communities across the Tulare Basin.
- **Published rate:** −17.7 to −34.5 cm/yr peak (2015–2017; up to −60 cm/yr during the 2015–2016 drought; >100 cm cumulative in UAVSAR 2013–2017; [NASA NISAR StoryMap](https://nisar.jpl.nasa.gov/applications/societal-benefit/)).
- **Our NISAR measurement:** −7.2 cm/100d LOS (−7.8 cm LOS, −10.1 cm vertical est., 2026-06-28 to 2026-10-14, 7 provisional pairs; 62.9% valid pixels, ref range [−7.8, −4.7] cm). An 11-month mixed chain separates −16.8 cm/yr permanent LOS trend from 5.8 cm seasonal oscillation and agrees with 10 NGL GPS stations (RMSE 1.0 cm, r = 0.68).
- **What NISAR adds:** 12-day L-band revisits track both active irrigation bowls and infrastructure corridors through crop growth.
