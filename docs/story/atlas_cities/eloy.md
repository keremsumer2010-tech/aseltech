# Eloy, USA (`eloy`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** High

- **Why it is sinking:** Long-term agricultural groundwater pumping in the Picacho Basin lowers hydraulic heads, compacting fine-grained alluvial aquitards and opening active earth fissures ([ADWR](https://www.azwater.gov/sites/default/files/2026-05/PicachoArea03-2025to03-2026_8x11.pdf) / USGS; Conway, 2016).
- **Who is affected:** Irrigated farmland, Interstate 10 and rail corridors, local roads, canals, and rural homes in Pinal County, Arizona.
- **Published rate:** >−5.7 m cumulative historical subsidence; >−7 cm/yr in localized active zones tracked by ADWR/USGS InSAR and GNSS (Conway, 2016).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary):** −6.7 cm/100d LOS (−8.0 cm LOS, −10.3 cm vertical est., 2026-06-19 to 2026-10-17, 9 pairs; 69.6% valid pixels, ref sensitivity [−8.4, −7.2] cm).
- **What NISAR adds:** High L-band coherence across arid agricultural fields resolves basin-wide summer compaction gradients and sharp fissure-margin transitions.
