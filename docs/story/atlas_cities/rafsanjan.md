# Rafsanjan, Iran (`rafsanjan`)

**Status:** Press-approved (PC5) · **Confidence:** Medium (`reliable`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Extensive groundwater pumping for pistachio irrigation in an arid terminal basin drains thick semi-confined alluvial clays, driving long-term inelastic compaction and destructive ground fissures ([Motagh et al., 2008](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-246X.2008.03786.x); [Haghighi & Motagh, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2023.113941)).
- **Who is affected:** Pistachio groves, transportation corridors, irrigation canal networks, and rural villages threatened by surface fissures.
- **Published rate:** 15 to 30+ cm/yr vertical subsidence across the Rafsanjan Plain (historical localized rates up to 37 cm/yr; [Motagh et al., 2008](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-246X.2008.03786.x); [Mousavi et al., 2001](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0264-3707(01)00030-9)).
- **Our NISAR measurement:** −4.8 cm/100d LOS (−5.8 cm LOS, −7.4 cm vertical est., 2026-06-23 to 2026-10-21, 120 days; 84.9% valid pixels, SNR = 17.6). Northern bedrock reference (56.236°E, 30.646°N) secures stable baseline calibration.
- **What NISAR adds:** Outstanding L-band coherence across hyper-arid terrain maps extraction bowl perimeters without vegetation decorrelation.
