# Jakarta, Indonesia (`jakarta`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Excessive groundwater extraction from compressible Holocene alluvium and clay, plus urban loads, drives rapid compaction ([Abidin et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11069-011-9866-9)).
- **Who is affected:** Coastal neighborhoods, seawalls, port facilities, and canal networks exposed to combined tidal and river flooding.
- **Published rate:** −1 to −15 cm/yr average (−7.3 to −7.7 cm/yr in recent InSAR/GNSS; historical hotspots −20 to −28 cm/yr; [Abidin et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11069-011-9866-9)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary, indicative only):** −6.3 cm/100d LOS (−6.8 cm LOS, −9.3 cm vertical est., 2026-06-27 to 2026-10-13, 8 pairs). Only 2.9%–3.0% of pixels remain valid due to tropical vegetation decorrelation, with one coastal reference window usable.
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band identifies localized coastal deformation pockets despite high humidity and urban decorrelation.
