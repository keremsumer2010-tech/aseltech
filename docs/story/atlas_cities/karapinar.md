# Karapınar, Konya, Türkiye (`karapinar`)

**Status:** Preliminary (PC5 conditional review) · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Intensive agricultural groundwater pumping lowers the water table in Neogene carbonate and alluvial sediments, driving both broad compaction and cover-collapse sinkholes ([Orhan et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/s21030774); [Üstün et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.5194/nhess-10-1151-2010)).
- **Who is affected:** Irrigated farmland, rural roads, power lines, and villages surrounding active sinkhole zones.
- **Published rate:** −1.2 to −5.2 cm/yr regional GNSS; >−7.0 cm/yr in agricultural bowls ([Orhan et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/s21030774)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (indicative only):** −5.5 cm/100d LOS (−5.9 cm LOS, −8.0 cm vertical est., 2026-06-26 to 2026-10-12, 8 provisional pairs; 14.2%–14.5% valid pixels, single reference). Across 29 mapped sinkholes, displacement averages −4.15 cm within 1 km versus −0.65 cm beyond 5 km (permutation p = 0.021).
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band radar detects broad summer subsidence gradients around karst-prone farmland where optical change is subtle.
