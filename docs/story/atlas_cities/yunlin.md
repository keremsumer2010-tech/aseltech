# Yunlin, Taiwan (`yunlin`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Intensive agricultural and aquaculture groundwater pumping across the Choushui River alluvial fan compacts deep unconsolidated clay and silt layers ([Hung et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhydrol.2010.03.005); Hwang et al., 2016).
- **Who is affected:** Over 650,000 residents, irrigated farmland, coastal aquaculture ponds, and Taiwan's High Speed Rail viaduct crossing Yunlin County.
- **Published rate:** −3 to −7 cm/yr across inland townships (Huwei, Tuku, Yuanchang), peaking above −7 cm/yr during dry pumping seasons ([Hung et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhydrol.2010.03.005)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary):** −2.5 cm/100d LOS (−2.7 cm LOS, −3.4 cm vertical est., 2026-06-28 to 2026-10-14, 108 days, 8 pairs; ref sensitivity [−3.5, −1.1] cm).
- **What NISAR adds:** 24 cm L-band radar penetrates persistent subtropical cloud cover and irrigated rice paddies to monitor differential ground settling along rail infrastructure.
