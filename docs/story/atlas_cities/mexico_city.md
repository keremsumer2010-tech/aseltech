# Mexico City, Mexico (`mexico_city`)

**Status:** Press-approved (PC5, after off-basin rerun) · **Confidence:** Medium

- **Why it is sinking:** Pumping from lacustrine clay aquitards of former Lake Texcoco consolidates highly compressible lakebed deposits ([Chaussard et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2013.10.004); [Cigna & Tapete, 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs13030477)).
- **Who is affected:** Metro lines, water mains, drainage tunnels, historic monuments, and millions of residents in eastern/northern boroughs.
- **Published rate:** −30 to −40 cm/yr vertical in lakebed bowls (historically up to −50 cm/yr); preliminary NISAR release (Oct 2025–Jan 2026) reported >2 cm/month.
- **Our NISAR measurement:** −5.8 cm/100d LOS (−7.0 cm LOS, −9.7 cm vertical est., 2026-06-22 to 2026-10-20, 8 pairs; 63.9% valid pixels). Re-referencing to the SW volcanic margin (−99.184°E, 19.377°N) eliminated an earlier in-basin reference bias (−4.4 cm).
- **What NISAR adds:** Standardized 120-day L-band mapping places Mexico City's compaction directly alongside global basins in one unified pipeline.
