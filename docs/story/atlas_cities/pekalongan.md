# Pekalongan, Indonesia — Rapid Coastal Sinking & QC Flag

**Driver & Risk:** Intensive groundwater pumping and alluvial clay compaction sink coastal Pekalongan below high-tide levels, driving chronic tidal flooding (*rob*) across dense neighborhoods, aquaculture ponds, and north-Java transport corridors.

**Literature:** Sidiq et al. ([2023](https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/1276/1/012014)) and Yuwono et al. ([2026](https://doi.org/10.22146/ijg.112793)) document 10–20 cm/yr (hotspots ~19–34.5 cm/yr) coastal subsidence.

**NISAR L-Band Measurement (`provisional`, `indicative only`):** Over 2026-06-20 to 2026-10-18 (9 pairs, 120 days), raw output reports **−22.2 cm** LOS (**−11.9 cm/100d** common; 2-ref −22.2 to −12.4 cm). Automated QC flagged a `phase_cycle_jump` (−13.03 cm on 2026-06-20→07-02, ~one 12.1 cm L-band cycle); excluding that step yields **−9.2 cm**, consistent with published rates.

**NISAR Value:** L-band tracks rapid Java coastal sinking under monsoon clouds while QC flags unwrapping jumps for clean reprocessing.

*NISAR data are provisional. Calibration and validation are carried out by the NISAR project (NASA-JPL and ISRO), not by our team.*
