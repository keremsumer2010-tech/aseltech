# Lagos, Nigeria — Coastal Fill & Lagoon-Edge Subsidence

**Driver & Risk:** Urban loading over reclaimed lagoonal sediments, soft coastal clays, and groundwater abstraction sinks low-lying Lagos districts, compounding storm-surge and drainage flooding for millions.

**Literature:** Ikuemonisan & Ozebo ([2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geog.2019.12.006)) combined SBAS-InSAR and GPS to map Lagos subsidence velocities from 0.2 to 8.7 cm/yr, peaking in coastal fill zones.

**NISAR L-Band Measurement (`provisional`):** Between 2026-06-24 and 2026-10-10 (8 pairs, 108 days), NISAR detects **−2.4 cm** peak 1 km LOS subsidence (**−2.1 cm/100d** common-window rate; −2.2 cm/100d full, −3.3 cm vertical estimate; 32.8% valid pixels; 2-ref range −2.4 to −2.4 cm; `medium` confidence, rank #14).

**NISAR Value:** L-band penetrates humid West African cloud cover and coastal vegetation to deliver identical two-reference estimates in a challenging tropical megacity.

*NISAR data are provisional. Calibration and validation are carried out by the NISAR project (NASA-JPL and ISRO), not by our team.*
