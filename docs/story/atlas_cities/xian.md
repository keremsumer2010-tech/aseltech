# Xi'an, China (`xian`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Historical groundwater over-pumping from confined aquifers compacted loess-alluvial layers and reactivated tectonic ground fissures (Qu et al., 2014; Zhang et al., 2018).
- **Who is affected:** Urban districts (Hi-tech, Qujiang, Chanba), metro tunnels, roads, and historic structures intersecting active ground-fissure zones.
- **Published rate:** Up to −13 cm/yr in the 1990s, ~−5 cm/yr (2005–2012 multi-band InSAR), slowing or rebounding (+2 cm/yr uplift) after groundwater controls and surface-water diversion (Qu et al., 2014; Zhang et al., 2018).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary, indicative only):** −1.2 cm/100d LOS (−1.5 cm LOS, −2.0 cm vertical est., 2026-06-19 to 2026-10-17, 9 pairs; 36.2% valid pixels, ref sensitivity [−3.9, −1.5] cm).
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band tracks subtle residual deformation and fissure-bounded gradients across dense urban blocks.
