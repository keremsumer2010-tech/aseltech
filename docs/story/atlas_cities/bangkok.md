# Bangkok, Thailand (`bangkok`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Deep well extraction from multi-layered Chao Phraya deltaic sand aquifers dewaters thick overlying soft marine clay (Bangkok Clay), compounded by heavy infrastructure loads ([Phien-wej et al., 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enggeo.2005.10.004); [Aobpaet et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jag.2012.10.005)).
- **Who is affected:** Over 10 million residents, Chao Phraya tidal floodwalls, drainage canal systems, and low-lying coastal industrial estates.
- **Published rate:** 0.5 to 1.0 cm/yr in central districts; 1 to 3 cm/yr in eastern industrial suburbs (historical 1980s peaks reached 10 to 12 cm/yr; [Phien-wej et al., 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enggeo.2005.10.004)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary, indicative only):** −6.25 cm/100d LOS (−7.5 cm LOS, −9.0 cm vertical est., 2026-06-21 to 2026-10-19, 120 days; 34.6% valid pixels). Tropical monsoon wet tropospheric delay likely inflates the short 120-day displacement signal 7–10× above multi-year literature rates.
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band retains partial coherence across wet tropical vegetation where C-band decorrelates, identifying candidate deformation pockets for dry-season verification.
