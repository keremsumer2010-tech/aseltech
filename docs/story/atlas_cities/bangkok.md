# Bangkok, Thailand (`bangkok`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Deep well extraction from deltaic sand aquifers dewaters thick overlying soft marine Bangkok Clay, compounded by surface construction loads ([Phien-wej et al., 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enggeo.2005.10.004); [Aobpaet et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jag.2012.10.005)).
- **Who is affected:** 10+ million residents, Chao Phraya floodwalls, drainage canals, and low-lying coastal industrial estates.
- **Published rate:** 0.5–1.0 cm/yr central; 1–3 cm/yr eastern industrial suburbs (1980s peaks: 10–12 cm/yr; [Phien-wej et al., 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enggeo.2005.10.004)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary, indicative only):** −6.25 cm/100d LOS (−7.5 cm LOS, −9.0 cm vertical est., 2026-06-21 to 2026-10-19, 120 days; 34.6% valid pixels). Monsoon wet tropospheric delay likely inflates this 120-day signal 7–10× above multi-year rates.
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band retains coherence across wet vegetation where C-band decorrelates, locating candidate deformation pockets for dry-season verification.
