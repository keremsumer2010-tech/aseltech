# Can Tho, Vietnam — Mekong Delta Urban Compaction

**Driver & Risk:** Urban loading, groundwater extraction, and compaction of soft Holocene clays sink Can Tho—the Mekong Delta's largest city—amplifying tidal and monsoon flood depth along embankments.

**Literature:** PSInSAR and field observations ([2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs13020189)) document 1.0–3.0+ cm/yr urban sinking (hotspots >4 cm/yr), matching regional ALOS PALSAR rates of 1–4 cm/yr ([2014](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/9/8/084010)).

**NISAR L-Band Measurement (`provisional`):** Over 2026-06-17 to 2026-10-15 (9 pairs, 120 days), NISAR detects **−3.4 cm** peak 1 km LOS subsidence (**−3.4 cm/100d** common-window rate; −2.8 cm/100d full, −4.4 cm vertical estimate; 2-ref range −3.4 to −3.1 cm; `medium` confidence, rank #11).

**NISAR Value:** Longer 24.2 cm L-band radar penetrates monsoon cloud cover and wet-rice/orchard canopies where C-band coherence drops, yielding a stable two-reference summer estimate.

*NISAR data are provisional. Calibration and validation are carried out by the NISAR project (NASA-JPL and ISRO), not by our team.*
