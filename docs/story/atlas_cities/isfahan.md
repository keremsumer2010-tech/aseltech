# Isfahan, Iran (`isfahan`)

**Status:** Press-approved (PC5) · **Confidence:** Medium

- **Why it is sinking:** Desiccation and damming of the Zayandeh-Rud River have forced severe agricultural and municipal groundwater overdraft, compressing unconfined and semi-confined alluvial clay-silt beds across the Isfahan-Borkhar basin ([Esmaeili et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhydrol.2023.129845); [Motagh et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jag.2017.06.002)).
- **Who is affected:** 2.2 million metropolitan inhabitants, UNESCO-registered historical bridges, airport infrastructure, and northern suburban rail corridors.
- **Published rate:** −5 to −11 cm/yr typical subsidence rate across the Isfahan-Borkhar plain ([Esmaeili et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhydrol.2023.129845)).
- **Our NISAR measurement:** −2.1 cm/100d LOS (−2.5 cm LOS, −3.4 cm vertical est., 2026-06-18 to 2026-10-16, 120 days; 32.5% valid pixels). Southern rocky-margin references show high stability at −2.5 to −2.6 cm LOS.
- **What NISAR adds:** 12-day L-band passes preserve phase continuity across arid urban peripheries, tracking subsidence gradients flanking historic cultural monuments.
