# Semarang, Indonesia (`semarang`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Medium

- **Why it is sinking:** Intensive industrial, commercial, and domestic groundwater extraction accelerates natural consolidation of soft Holocene coastal alluvium and clay under heavy urban loads (Abidin et al., 2013; Bott et al., 2021).
- **Who is affected:** Northern coastal neighborhoods, Tanjung Emas Port, Semarang Tawang rail corridor, and industrial estates exposed to chronic tidal flooding (*rob*).
- **Published rate:** −6 to −9 cm/yr spatial average across low-lying coastal zones, reaching −10 to −19 cm/yr in northern hotspots (Abidin et al., 2013; Bott et al., 2021).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary):** −11.2 cm/100d LOS (−12.1 cm LOS, −15.5 cm vertical est., 2026-06-25 to 2026-10-11, 8 pairs; ref sensitivity [−15.7, −12.1] cm).
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band penetrates humid coastal foliage and dense urban clutter to map active coastal subsidence bowls every 12 days.
