# Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam (`ho_chi_minh`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Heavy municipal and industrial groundwater extraction from multi-layer Mekong–Dong Nai deltaic aquifers dewaters highly compressible Holocene soft marine clays, aggravated by rapid urban surface construction ([Erban et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/9/8/084010); [Minderhoud et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-21-509-2017)).
- **Who is affected:** 9+ million metropolitan residents, drainage canal networks, low-lying tidal floodplains along the Saigon River, and southern port facilities.
- **Published rate:** 2 to 5 cm/yr typical urban subsidence, with localized industrial hotspots reaching 7 to 8 cm/yr ([Erban et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/9/8/084010); [Minderhoud et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-21-509-2017)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary, indicative only):** −3.5 cm/100d LOS (−4.2 cm LOS, −5.8 cm vertical est., 2026-06-17 to 2026-10-15, 120 days, 9 provisional pairs). Reference sensitivity spans [−9.9, −4.2] cm LOS across two reference points, reflecting tropical deltaic baseline variability.
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band microwave imaging penetrates tropical monsoon cloud covers and wetland vegetation along the delta margin where optical sensors fail.
