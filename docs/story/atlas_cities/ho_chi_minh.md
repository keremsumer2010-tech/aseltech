# Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam (`ho_chi_minh`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Groundwater extraction from multi-layer deltaic aquifers dewaters compressible Holocene clays, compounded by urban loading ([Erban et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/9/8/084010); [Minderhoud et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-21-509-2017)).
- **Who is affected:** 9+ million residents, drainage canals, tidal floodplains along Saigon River, and port facilities.
- **Published rate:** 2–5 cm/yr typical urban subsidence; industrial hotspots reach 7–8 cm/yr ([Erban et al., 2014](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/9/8/084010); [Minderhoud et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-21-509-2017)).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary, indicative only):** −3.5 cm/100d LOS (−4.2 cm LOS, −5.8 cm vertical est., 2026-06-17 to 2026-10-15, 120 days, 9 pairs). Reference sensitivity spans [−9.9, −4.2] cm LOS across two reference points.
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band microwaves penetrate tropical monsoon clouds and wetland vegetation along delta margins where optical sensors fail.
