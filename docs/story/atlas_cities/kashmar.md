# Kashmar Valley, Iran — Agricultural Aquifer Compaction

**Driver & Risk:** Intensive groundwater pumping for irrigated agriculture in the Kashmar–Bardaskan valley compacts alluvial clay-silt beds bounded by old alpine basement faults, threatening rural irrigation canals, roads, and basin settlements.

**Literature:** Anderssohn et al. ([2008](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-246X.2008.03805.x)) mapped fault-controlled valley subsidence historically reaching 15–30 cm/yr using InSAR and leveling.

**NISAR L-Band Measurement (`provisional`):** Between 2026-06-19 and 2026-10-17 (9 pairs, 120 days), NISAR records peak 1 km LOS subsidence of **−6.2 cm** (**−7.0 cm/100d** common-window rate; −5.2 cm/100d full, −7.6 cm vertical estimate; 72.8% valid pixels; 2-ref range −7.0 to −6.2 cm; `high` confidence, rank #4).

**NISAR Value:** L-band maintains high coherence over dry agricultural soils, tracking active fault-bounded drawdown across the valley without C-band decorrelation.

*NISAR data are provisional. Calibration and validation are carried out by the NISAR project (NASA-JPL and ISRO), not by our team.*
