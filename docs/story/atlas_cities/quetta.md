# Quetta, Pakistan (`quetta`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Medium

- **Why it is sinking:** Unplanned deep-aquifer tube-well pumping for agriculture and urban supply drops water tables by 1.5–5.0 m/yr, compacting unconsolidated valley-fill sediments (Kakar et al., 2016; Khan et al., 2013).
- **Who is affected:** Central Quetta urban neighborhoods, roads, water pipelines, buildings, and surrounding agricultural districts in Balochistan.
- **Published rate:** ~−3 cm/yr on valley flanks and −12 to −16.6 cm/yr in the central urban core from Sentinel-1 InSAR and GNSS (Kakar et al., 2016).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary):** −3.2 cm/100d LOS (−3.8 cm LOS, −5.5 cm vertical est., 2026-06-17 to 2026-10-15, 9 pairs; 71.3% valid pixels, ref sensitivity [−7.9, −3.3] cm).
- **What NISAR adds:** Strong arid-basin L-band coherence (71.3% valid pixels) tracks deformation across both the urban core and surrounding agricultural valley floor.
