# Kerman, Iran — Arid Basin Overdraft

**Driver & Risk:** Groundwater extraction for pistachio orchards and urban demand drains confined alluvial aquifers in the Kerman basin, inducing earth fissures and straining highways and pipelines.

**Literature:** Nationwide Sentinel-1 InSAR (2014–2020) by Haghshenas Haghighi & Motagh ([2024](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adp0896)) mapped subsidence exceeding 35 cm/yr across Kerman Province's steepest depression cones.

**NISAR L-Band Measurement (`provisional`):** Over 2026-06-19 to 2026-10-17 (9 pairs, 120 days), NISAR measures peak 1 km LOS subsidence of **−6.1 cm** (**−5.3 cm/100d** common-window rate; −5.1 cm/100d full, −7.8 cm vertical estimate; 96.8% valid pixels; 2-ref range −6.1 to −5.6 cm; `high` confidence, rank #8).

**NISAR Value:** Near-complete spatial coherence (96.8% valid) and tight formal uncertainty (`σ = 0.258 cm`) resolve basin-scale compaction gradients in a single 12-day repeat stack.

*NISAR data are provisional. Calibration and validation are carried out by the NISAR project (NASA-JPL and ISRO), not by our team.*
