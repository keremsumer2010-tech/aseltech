# Delhi, India (`delhi`)

**Status:** Preliminary · **Confidence:** Low (`indicative only`)

- **Why it is sinking:** Unchecked borewell groundwater depletion in compressible Quaternary alluvial aquifers drops pore pressure, causing permanent clay-layer compaction (Garg et al., 2022).
- **Who is affected:** Dense urban districts in Southwest Delhi (including Kapashera), surrounding Haryana fringe towns, and corridors near Indira Gandhi International Airport.
- **Published rate:** ~−11 cm/yr (2014–2016) accelerating to −15 to −17 cm/yr (2016–2018) in Southwest Delhi hotspots, with ~+2 cm/yr localized rebound where recharge/regulation took effect (Garg et al., 2022).
- **Our NISAR measurement (preliminary, indicative only):** −3.6 cm/100d LOS (−4.3 cm LOS, −5.4 cm vertical est., 2026-06-21 to 2026-10-19, 8 pairs; 5.3% valid pixels, ref sensitivity [−13.5, −1.5] cm).
- **What NISAR adds:** L-band detects localized deformation pockets even under monsoon vegetation cover, though low valid-pixel coverage requires longer multi-season stacks.
