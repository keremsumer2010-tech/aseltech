## 3. Top 5 Jury Questions — One-Sentence Answers (`docs/story/judge_faq.md`)

**Q&A Leads:** **Mehmet** (SAR math, orbits, atmosphere, GPS validation) & **İbrahim** (infrastructure risk, sinkholes, London negative control, simulator & open access).

1. **Q1 (`FAQ #1`): Are your NISAR measurements fully calibrated science products, and why mix beta data in the full-year view?**  
   *Answer (Mehmet):* Every main science layer uses NASA-ISRO `provisional` scenes (`CRID P05023`, `17 Jun 2026` onward, calibrated by NASA-JPL and ISRO, not by us), while our exploratory 11-month view **includes pre-calibration data** (`18` pairs before `28 Jun 2026`), lives in a separate `mixed` folder, and is drawn dashed with explicit caution labels.
2. **Q2 (`FAQ #2`): Why report slanted Line-of-Sight (`LOS`) numbers instead of pure vertical ground sinking?**  
   *Answer (Mehmet):* InSAR measures one-dimensional motion along the radar beam (`39.1°` in California, `41.9°` in Karapınar), so we keep base maps strictly in LOS, label `LOS / cos θ` conversions as estimates, and confirm `-7.2 cm` vertical sinking (`-0.1 cm` east-west) at Alpaugh by combining ascending Track 34 with descending Track 114.
3. **Q3 (`FAQ #3 & #4`): How do you keep atmospheric vapour and phase-unwrapping glitches from creating fake hotspots?**  
   *Answer (Mehmet):* On every pair our tested pipeline subtracts the GUNW ionosphere screen and an elevation-aware plane, drops low-coherence (`< 0.3`), disconnected, or half-cycle-jumping pixels, and reports `1 km` block medians checked against `~0.3 cm` radar noise and a `3.62 cm` London clay negative control.
4. **Q4 (`FAQ #8`): How closely do satellite radar measurements match independent GPS stations on the ground?**  
   *Answer (Mehmet):* Across 10 continuous Nevada Geodetic Laboratory GPS stations inside the Central Valley frame, NISAR vertical estimates match daily GPS positions with a `1.00 cm` median time-series RMSE (`0.76 cm` final RMSE at the 4 fastest-sinking interior stations and `r = 0.924` cross-station correlation).
5. **Q5 (`FAQ #7 & #5`): Can NISAR predict the date or location of the next Karapınar sinkhole collapse?**  
   *Answer (İbrahim):* No — NISAR tracks multi-pixel groundwater drawdown rather than day-ahead cavity roof failure, though land within `1 km` of 29 documented Karapınar sinkholes sank `6.4 times` faster (`-4.15 cm` LOS) than farmland beyond `5 km` (`-0.65 cm`), pinpointing shared aquifer stress.
