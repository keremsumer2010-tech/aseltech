> **Challenge Alignment (`A5`):** Space Apps *"Dancing with the SARs"* asks how open NISAR change maps provide **actionable insights** for scientists, water & canal managers, educators, decision-makers, and the general public.  
> **Mandatory Calibration Notice:** *NISAR data are provisional. Calibration and validation are carried out by the NISAR project (NASA-JPL and ISRO), not by our team.*

## 1. Scientists & Geodesists
Scientists find reproducible Python InSAR processing scripts (`analysis/insar.py`, `cumulative.py`), per-pixel Cramér-Rao formal phase uncertainty estimates (`0.294 cm` median in the Central Valley, `0.326 cm` in Karapınar, [`docs/story/uncertainty.md`](uncertainty.md)), a 10-station continuous GPS validation benchmark (`r = 0.924` final cross-station correlation, `1.00 cm` median time-series RMSE, [`docs/story/validation_report.md`](validation_report.md)), dual-orbit ascending Track 34 versus descending Track 114 cross-checks (`6,719` 1 km blocks, `85%` sign agreement, `data/central_valley/vertical.json`), and an 11-month `25`-pair harmonic decomposition (`quality: "mixed"`, [`docs/story/fullyear.md`](fullyear.md)). They can inspect ionospheric and topographic-ramp residuals directly, compare 24 cm L-band agricultural coherence against C-band datasets ([Neely et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8047915/), [Orhan et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7865528/), [`docs/story/cross_sensor.md`](cross_sensor.md)), and reproduce or extend the processing chain on incoming NISAR `GUNW` granules via [ASF Vertex](https://search.asf.alaska.edu/) and the [ASF NISAR Data User Guide](https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/gunw).

## 2. Water & Canal Managers
Water district engineers and canal operators find differential tilt profiles across `645` OpenStreetMap infrastructure corridors (`81` flagged high-risk in `data/central_valley/assets.json` and `10` in `data/karapinar/assets.json`, [`docs/story/risk_dossier.md`](risk_dossier.md)), led by the `Tulare Irrigation District Canal` (`57.01 mm/km` over `2.17 km`) and `102.8 km` of the `California Aqueduct` (`49.12 mm/km`)—updating the `2013–2017` airborne UAVSAR aqueduct baseline featured in [NASA's NISAR Land Subsidence StoryMap](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/109e602e748346f3be8dc89124619828) with 2026 spaceborne observations. Using our Top-10 rank-invariant priority list (`±25%` threshold sensitivity in [`docs/story/risk_sensitivity.md`](risk_sensitivity.md)) and the 24-month **Valley Under Pressure** simulator (`game/index.html`), managers can target field leveling crews before differential settling cuts gravity-flow capacity (as seen on the Friant-Kern Canal, [KVPR 2017](https://www.kvpr.org/environment/2017-08-03/friant-kern-canal-slows-by-60-percent-subsidence-to-blame)) and test pumping reductions that keep water heads above irreversible compaction limits.

## 3. Educators & Students
High-school and university instructors find a ready-to-teach 40-minute classroom lesson plan ([`docs/story/lesson_plan.md`](lesson_plan.md)), an interactive cloud-versus-radar slider over eastern England (`data/uk/meta.json`), a browser 3D surface explorer (`web3d/`), and Classroom Mode scenarios inside **Valley Under Pressure** paired with [NASA Earthdata's Intro to SAR](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/earth-observation-data-basics/sar) and [NASA ARSET trainings](https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/get-involved/training). Educators can guide students through a self-contained browser lab measuring how 24 cm radar waves see beneath clouds, distinguishing recoverable seasonal elasticity (`11.7 cm` peak-to-peak near Earlimart) from permanent clay compaction (`−16.8 cm/yr`), and grading five quantitative questions with an included answer key.

## 4. Decision-Makers & Infrastructure Planners
Policy boards, California Groundwater Sustainability Agencies (GSAs), and Konya–Karapınar planners find common-pixel subsidence footprints (`239.54 km² <= −5 cm` and `1,578.27 km² <= −2 cm` in the Central Valley; `3.10 km² <= −5 cm` and `28.66 km² <= −2 cm` in Karapınar, `data/*/area_stats.json`), exposed population and crop totals ([`docs/story/exposure.md`](exposure.md)), and statistically confirmed karst risk zones (`0–1 km` ring around `29` verified sinkholes subsiding `6.4×` faster than `> 5 km` background, `p = 0.0001`, [`docs/story/sinkhole_stats.md`](sinkhole_stats.md)). Informed by the [NISAR Societal Benefits White Papers](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/societal-benefits/), officials can use these mapped zones to prioritize well metering, drought recharge allocations, and buffer inspections near high-tilt roads such as Highway `42-42` (`19.74 mm/km`, `597 m` from `Köken Obruğu-II`).

## 5. General Public & Farming Communities
Residents, farmers, and non-specialist visitors find a jargon-free summary ([`docs/story/key_findings_public.md`](key_findings_public.md)), a 30-second subtitled explainer video ([`docs/story/media/demo_30s.mp4`](media/demo_30s.mp4)), tangible historical references such as the `1851` Holme Fen cast-iron post (`nearly 4 m` exposed at `2.75 m` below sea level, [`docs/story/holme_fen.md`](holme_fen.md)), and interactive click cards in [`web/index.html`](../../web/index.html) that convert radar phase shifts into plain sentences. Any visitor with a browser can inspect summer 2026 ground sinking near local communities, read verified histories of the `29` Karapınar sinkholes ([`docs/story/sinkhole_timeline.md`](sinkhole_timeline.md)), and understand why conserving deep aquifer pore pressure protects shared roads, canals, and harvests.
