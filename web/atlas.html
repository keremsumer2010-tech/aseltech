How to read this map (4 steps)
  1. LOS vs. vertical: Numbers show radar line-of-sight (LOS) motion; use Values: LOS to toggle approximate vertical (LOS / cos θ).
  2. Color scale: Deeper red marks faster sinking (cm or cm/100d over the 93-day common window); pale tones mark near-zero motion.
  3. Confidence badge: high / medium badges indicate consistent multi-reference stacks and solid valid coverage (reliable).
  4. Indicative only: Low-coverage scenes or QC-flagged series (phase-cycle jumps or single-date spikes) are separated as indicative only.
