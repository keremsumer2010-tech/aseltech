How we used AI

Code, analysis scripts and text were written with the help of AI assistants (Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini) and checked by the team: every number comes from the data files, every scientific claim has a source, and tests cover the core conversions. No AI-generated image is presented as data; maps show only real measurements.
